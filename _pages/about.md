I am a Computer Science PhD Candidate at <a href='https://www.uniroma1.it/en'>Sapienza University of Rome</a>, where I am part of the <a href='https://www.pinlab.org/'>Perception and Intelligence Laboratory (PINlab)</a>, supervised by <a href='https://fgalasso.bitbucket.io'>Professor Fabio Galasso</a>. I also work with <a href='https://warmwind.space/'>WarmWind AG</a> in Jena as a Machine Learning Engineer.

My research focuses on <b>Socially Aware Robotics</b>, <b>Human-Robot Interaction</b>, and <b>Embodied AI</b>, with a background in <b>Computer Vision</b>, <b>Deep Reinforcement Learning</b>, and applied machine learning systems.

I am interested in building robots and agents that reason about people, communicate when useful, and act robustly in real-world environments. My current work bridges academic research and production-oriented machine learning engineering across robotics, world models, and embodied intelligence.
